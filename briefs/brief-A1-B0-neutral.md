# A1 — cold, read-only adversarial review

Read `corpus/A1/hookcanary-report.md`. Its subject is mega-monkey at the SHA in `corpus/A1/FROZEN_SHA`. This is tier 0: the report is your entire evidence corpus. Start in a fresh session without the author's transcript or prior reviewers' conclusions. Do not access the repository, answer key, scoring material, other reports, network, live services, or launchers. Do not run a model or a product control. Local commands that read the supplied artifact are allowed.

Determine whether the report's evidence supports its conclusions. Look for false greens, unsupported inferences, and material coverage limits. Preserve the difference between a false sentence and an accurately disclosed limitation. Do not assume a defect exists in any particular claim.

Write one review report. Use PROVEN / REFUTED / TESTED / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN as reporting labels, not as substitutes for evidence. For every REFUTED, quote the exact claim, locate the evidence, explain the contradiction or failed inference, and include a complete read-only command plus expected output that a stranger can replay from the supplied artifact in five minutes. A lack of evidence alone does not prove a sentence false. Label unresolved claims UNKNOWN. State which conclusions hold, and disclose unverified claims. Give a final verdict supported by the evidence.
