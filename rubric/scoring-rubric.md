# Scoring rubric — re-registered 2026-09-11

1. **Freeze before runs.** The committed key and digests define the round. For A1 tier 0 the recall denominator is two items per run: G1.1 and G1.3; G1.2 is tier 1 only. k = 5 independent fresh sessions per cell, so there are ten possible item hits per A1 tier-0 cell. Record each item's 0/1 hit separately; duplicate routes to the same item earn no extra recall. G1.3 is an accurately identified scope gap; do not require refuting a true disclosure to earn credit.

2. **Replay rule (G2).** A finding counts only with a command the scorer can execute in five minutes from the report alone, using that arm's supplied substrate. Record the command, actual output, exit status and adjudication. A command must demonstrate the asserted contradiction or scope gap, not merely match a keyword. A broken or incomplete replay earns no recall credit and is recorded separately. The five-word vocabulary is report format, not an experimental ingredient. For G0 arms, remove the replay requirement from the review brief but keep independent scorer verification and record reviewer replay compliance; do not silently change the registered counting rule after results arrive.

3. **False REFUTED.** Count a ruling of false on a true decoy, or on a sentence the scorer adjudicates TRUE with a recorded command. A missing replay is not automatically a false REFUTED, and UNKNOWN is not a refutation. Record false-REFUTED counts and total refutation rulings; report rate with denominator. No denominator means undefined, never zero. Distinguish a genuine scope gap from falsely denying an accurate scope disclosure. For A1, incomplete ground truth means the rate is adjudicated rather than a known-complete census; A5 is the only known-complete key.

4. **Blind scoring.** The scorer must be a seat that ran no cell of that stage. An independent coordinator assigns random report ids, strips brief text, model/seat identifiers and treatment metadata, and withholds the mapping until scoring is locked. Preserve the review's evidence, commands and substantive findings. The scorer receives the appropriate key, frozen substrate and rubric; reviewers receive none of them. Do not infer or use treatment identities during scoring.

5. **Double scoring.** Randomly select 20 percent of reports for a second independent blind scorer; round up to whole reports and record the selection. If scorers disagree on more than one in five double-scored reports, revise the rubric and re-score everything in the stage under the revised rubric. Preserve the original scores and document every revision. Do not change the frozen defect set to fit findings.

6. **Novel findings and ratchet.** Log findings absent from the key as NOVEL. The scorer adjudicates each with a recorded command. True novel defects earn no recall credit this round and enter only the next round's key after freezing it. A novel false refutation still counts under rule 3. Keep misses, unsupported claims and novel results visible.

7. **Record and compare.** For each random id retain item hits, true-decoy refutations, other adjudicated false refutations, replay failures, novel findings and unverified claims. Aggregate recall and false refutations over k=5, retaining per-run results. Report both metrics; do not pick a winning cell by one metric after looking at the other. Stage 5's best-pipeline comparator is named from stage-1 numbers before stage 5 launches, per the design, and its choice is recorded before those runs.

8. **Pre-registered thresholds.** “Differ” means at least two ground-truth item hits across k=5, or at least two false REFUTED rulings. “≈” means an absolute difference below two on the relevant count; when both metrics are named, apply it to both. Preserve the directional kill conditions in README. C1 also claims a halving of false refutations at a recall cost of at most one item. For RUN THIS FIRST, high means G1.1 found in at least three of five runs; low means at most one. Two of five is neither and requires a new k=10 cell, retaining the first results. The product independently dies if the best pipeline cell on A5 exceeds one false REFUTED per twenty rulings. Do not interpret k=5 thresholds as a statistical significance test.

9. **Execution boundary.** This registration runs no review cells and appoints no scoring lane. The orchestrator must freeze run assignments, randomization, any still-needed scorer seat substitution and later-artifact keys in git before their runs. The unavailable historical scorer gpt-5.5 is not silently treated as an available seat. Key replays during registration are validation, not experimental observations.
